# Power-BI---ABC-Ecommerce-Sales-Dashboard


Power Bi Dashboard from Raw Data for Ecommerce Sales


An interactive E-commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard built in Power BI to analyze sales performance, customer trends, order distributions, and product profitability across regions and categories for ABC E-commerce.

**Project Overview**


This Power BI project equips sales managers, category managers, and business leaders with key insights into revenue drivers, order volumes, profit margins, and regional performance. The interactive dashboard allows stakeholders to evaluate historical trends, inspect category-level breakdown, and drill down into individual transaction details.


**Data Model & DAX Measures**


The report utilizes an optimized star-schema data model connecting sales transactions, customer demographics, product categories, and geographical locations.


Key DAX Measures developed for this report include:

* Total Sales
* Total Quantity Sold
* Total Profit
* Profit Margin
* Average Order Value (AOV)


**Dashboard Layout & Key Visualizations**

The dashboard is built using a dark-themed visual layout (Fluent2 design framework) featuring high contrast highlights for key performance indicators:


1. Executive Key Performance Indicators (KPI BANs)

    Total Revenue: Comprehensive summary of gross revenue.
  
    Total Orders & Units Sold: Volume tracking across all order channels.
  
    Overall Profit Margin: Quick assessment of business profitability.
  
    Average Order Value (AOV): Key metric evaluating customer basket size.


2. Sales Performance & Trend Analysis

    Monthly Revenue & Profit Trends: Line/Bar combo visual tracking monthly performance against targets.
  
    Category & Sub-Category Performance: Breakdown of sales and profit margins across key product lines (Electronics, Clothing, Home & Living, etc.).
  
    Geographic Distribution: Interactive map and regional bar chart identifying top-performing regions and fulfillment zones.
  
    Payment & Delivery Mode Analysis: Pie/Donut breakdown of customer checkout preferences (Credit Card, UPI, COD, Net Banking) and shipping modes.



**Key Features & Interactivity**

  Cross-Filtering & Drill-Down: Click on any product category, region, or payment method to dynamically filter all visual cards across the dashboard.

  Slicers & Global Controls: Multi-select slicers for Date Ranges, Regions, Product Categories, and Customer Segments.

  Custom Theme: Dark gradient background design tuned for dark mode readability and modern aesthetic appeal.



**Tools & Technologies Used**

Business Intelligence Tool: Microsoft Power BI Desktop

Data Transformation: Power Query (M Language)

Analytical Calculations: Data Analysis Expressions (DAX)
